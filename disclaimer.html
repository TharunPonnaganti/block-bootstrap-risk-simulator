Calculation Method — Drik Ganita

Our calculations follow the Drik Ganita (modern astronomical observation-based) system using the Astronomy Engine library (VSOP87 planetary models) with Chitrapaksha (Lahiri) Ayanamsa correction. This is the same approach used by widely-referenced online Panchangam sources.

Subtle differences may arise compared to printed or traditional Panchangam books due to:

  • Drik Ganita vs Surya Siddhanta — Traditional Telugu Panchangam publications may use the ancient Surya Siddhanta model or Vakya Panchangam tables, which can differ from modern Drik Ganita calculations by several minutes (and occasionally produce different Tithi or Nakshatra names for a given day).
  • Ayanamsa variations — Different publishers use different ayanamsa systems (Lahiri, Raman, Krishnamurti, etc.), leading to slight shifts in Nakshatra, Yoga, and Karana boundaries.
  • Regional month systems — We follow the southern Amanta (new-moon-ending) lunisolar system standard in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Purnimanta (full-moon-ending) sources used in North India will show different Telugu Masa assignments for the same date.
  • Local temple traditions and sampradaya-specific practices that may observe festivals on different dates.

Sunrise Tithi Convention

This site displays the Tithi and Nakshatra active at local sunrise as the primary value for each day. This is the standard convention used in Drik Ganita-based Panchangams.

Tithi Kshaya & Vriddhi

Occasionally, a Tithi or Nakshatra may start after one sunrise and end before the next sunrise. This is known as Tithi Kshaya (or Nakshatra Kshaya) — the Tithi is astronomically “lost” and does not appear as any day’s primary Tithi. Conversely, a slow-moving Tithi may span two consecutive sunrises (Tithi Vriddhi), appearing on two days.

These are natural astronomical phenomena caused by variations in the Moon’s orbital speed. They are not errors. Different Panchangam traditions handle these cases differently, which is another reason values may appear to differ between sources.

Festival Dates

Festival dates shown are computed algorithmically based on lunar month and Tithi rules. Complex observances that depend on regional traditions, panchanga committee decisions, or temple-specific calendars may differ from what is shown here. Always verify important festival dates with your local temple or Pandit.
