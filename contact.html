Frequently Asked Questions

Why does your Panchangam differ from my local calendar? Different publishers use different ayanamsa systems (Lahiri, Raman, Krishnamurti). We use Lahiri (Chitrapaksha), which is the Indian Government standard. Small differences of 1-2 minutes in tithi boundaries are normal between sources.

Can I request a new city? Absolutely! Send us the city name, country, and approximate coordinates if you have them. We typically add new cities within one week of receiving a request.

Is the site free to use? Yes, Telugu Panchangam Daily is completely free. We sustain the site through non-intrusive advertising. All astronomical calculations run in your browser with no server costs, login requirements, or premium tiers.

Do you offer an API or widget? Not currently, but we are open to partnerships. If you run a Telugu community website, temple site, or cultural app and would like to integrate Panchangam data, please reach out to discuss options.
