What Is Telugu Panchangam Daily?

Telugu Panchangam Daily is a free Vedic Hindu calendar that provides daily Panchangam details for the global Telugu community. Whether you are in Hyderabad, Dallas, London, or Sydney, you can view computed astronomical data for your city and date.

What We Provide

  • Daily Panchangam — Tithi, Nakshatra (Star), Yogam, Karanam with computed start and end times
  • Sunrise & Sunset — Computed for your selected city using the VSOP87 astronomical model
  • Auspicious Timings — Abhijit Muhurtham, Brahma Muhurtham, and Amritha Kalam
  • Inauspicious Timings — Rahu Kalam, Gulika Kalam, and Yamagandam
  • Monthly Calendar — Full-month view with Tithi and Nakshatra for every day
  • Festival Dates — Pournami, Amavasya, Ekadashi, Pradosham, and major Telugu festivals
  • 70+ Cities — Covering India, USA, Canada, UK/Europe, Middle East, Australia, and Asia

How It Works

All calculations are performed entirely in your browser using the Astronomy Engine library, a well-tested, open-source astronomical library built on the VSOP87 planetary model. We apply the Lahiri ayanamsa correction for sidereal positioning, which is the standard used by the Indian government's official Rashtriya Panchang.

All Panchangam calculations run locally in your browser — no external Panchangam API or server is used for the astronomical data. The page does load Google Fonts and advertising scripts from external servers, but the Panchangam computations themselves are entirely client-side.

Why We Built This

The Telugu community is spread across the world. Finding city-specific Panchangam for locations outside India is difficult. Most existing calendars either cover only Indian cities or rely on pre-computed tables that cannot adjust for your selected city. We built Telugu Panchangam Daily to solve this — a single page that computes real-time, city-specific Panchangam using astronomy, not static tables.

Accuracy & Disclaimer

Our calculations are based on well-tested astronomical models and are computed for reference. However, traditional Panchangam can vary between different schools and almanacs, and small differences in timing are normal. Always consult a qualified Pandit for important muhurtham decisions such as weddings, griha pravesham, and other sacred ceremonies.
