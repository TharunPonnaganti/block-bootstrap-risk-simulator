Overview

Telugu Panchangam Daily ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The site does not require registration, login, or any form submission to use. All Panchangam calculations run entirely in your browser — no data is sent to our servers.

City Selection

When you select a city, the coordinates are used locally in your browser to compute astronomical data. Your city selection is not transmitted to any server, stored in any database, or shared with any third party.

Cookies & Local Storage

Telugu Panchangam Daily uses your browser's localStorage to remember your preferred city so you can quickly access your city's Panchangam on return visits. This data is stored only in your browser and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear this preference at any time using the "Change city" link on the homepage or by clearing your browser's local storage.

Third-party services embedded on our site may also use cookies as described below.

Geolocation (Use My Location)

If you choose to use the "Use My Location" feature, your browser will ask for permission to access your device's GPS coordinates. This location data is used only in your browser to find the nearest supported city from our list. Your coordinates are never transmitted to our servers, stored in any database, or shared with any third party. You can deny or revoke location access at any time through your browser settings.

Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google and its advertising partners, as third-party vendors, use cookies — including the DoubleClick DART cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites across the internet. Web beacons (small transparent images) may also be used by ad networks to record ad impressions and interactions.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

For more information about how Google uses data when you use our site, please review How Google Uses Information from Sites That Use Its Services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare

Our site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance purposes. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google may log your IP address. See Google Fonts Privacy.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at TeluguPanchangamdaily@gmail.com.
